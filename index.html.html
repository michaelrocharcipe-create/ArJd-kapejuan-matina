Project Background

Solving the Matina Rush Hour Bottleneck

KapeJuan Matina is conceptualized to address the heavy foot traffic and congestion experienced right outside St. John Paul II Parish and neighboring academic zones during peak hours. Patrons often wait 20 to 30 minutes in line just to place coffee orders after religious services or afternoon classes.

By implementing this lightweight pre-order dashboard, customers can secure their beverages and pastries ahead of time, eliminating physical queue crowding and supporting local Mindanao coffee growers efficiently.
